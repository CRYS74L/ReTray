The checked-in SVG paths are the selected library assets. Ionicons uses an open stroked arc and a separate vertical line, with inline styles and round caps; Phosphor Light uses filled outline paths. P3 startup preserves the approved preview calibration: its original 512 grid is rendered at 176 within a 192 viewport, with stroke width 26.212765957 source units. This is encoded as viewBox -23.2727272727 -23.2727272727 558.5454545454 558.5454545454 and rendered in a 21.5 DIP viewport (7.5 percent larger than the initial faithful 20 DIP P3 rendering, as subsequently requested by the user), without boolean erosion of its arc. The other three glyphs retain their existing ink normalization and one DIP outline compensation. Final ink stays centered in the unchanged 28 DIP icon column. Geometry, not font codepoints, supplies the shapes. Color follows the active text brush.
